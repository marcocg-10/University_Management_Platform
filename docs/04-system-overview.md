# 4. System Overview

## Context and Current Situation

The **University of Costa Rica (UCR)**, as a *public institution of higher education*, faces a constant challenge: ensuring **equitable and high-quality access** to professional education for all students across the country. However, factors such as **geographical distance**, **limited infrastructure**, and the **concentration of resources** in certain campuses prevent equal opportunities for all students from the university experience.

To explore innovative ways of bridging these gaps and, at the same time, to showcase the richness of university life, the UCR has launched an initiative called **ThemePark@UCR**.  
This project envisions a **Web and Virtual Reality (VR) application** that recreates the essence of the *Rodrigo Facio Brenes University City* as an interactive, three-dimensional **“theme park”**.  

> Rather than simply providing static information, the platform aims to **immerse prospective and newly admitted students**—as well as the general public—into a vivid representation of **daily academic and cultural life** at the university.

Within this virtual environment, users will be able to:

- **Navigate a detailed 3D map** of key university sites.  
- **Explore the facilities** of different schools and faculties.  
- **Access educational resources** located in virtual learning spaces.  
- Enjoy **gamification elements** (missions, rankings, collaborative activities).  
- **Interact simultaneously** with other users.  

These interactions will serve different audiences:  
- **Future students** → seeking career information.  
- **Current students** → familiarizing themselves with campus services.  
- **Visitors** → learning about the university’s research and cultural contributions.  

While the immediate purpose of **ThemePark@UCR** is to enrich **outreach and orientation efforts**, the project also reflects a **forward-looking vision**.  
By experimenting with **metaverse technologies** and **immersive learning**, the university is laying the groundwork for long-term possibilities such as a truly **virtual university experience**.

In this way, the initiative not only **promotes the university’s academic offerings and values** but also represents a **first step toward democratizing access to education** and strengthening **UCR’s role as a leader in innovation and knowledge sharing** across Costa Rica and the wider region.

## Problem Statement

In the current context of higher education in Costa Rica, a significant gap persists between the academic potential offered by the University of Costa Rica and the ability of all students in the country to access this educational experience equitably.  

This disparity is evident not only in access to professional training, but also in the overall university experience: understanding the campus environment, its services, cultural activities, and extracurricular opportunities.  

Geographical limitations, the concentration of resources on the main campus, and the lack of interactive orientation mechanisms make it difficult for students from remote areas or with fewer resources to visualize and connect with university life before enrolling.  

**ThemePark@UCR** emerges as an innovative solution to this problem, through the development of a web and virtual reality platform that recreates the Rodrigo Facio Brenes University Campus as an interactive 3D environment.

The University wants to provide an immersive, interactive, and research-capable Virtual Reality gateway (**ThemePark@UCR**) for:

- **Learning**
- **Entertainment**
- **Discovery**

Currently:

1. Student use of interactive educational tools is scattered over separate platforms.  
2. Administrative work is manual or via IA agent.  
3. Researchers lack a shared environment in which they can harvest behavior or interaction data securely.  

Existing campus infrastructure (learning management, content delivery, authentication) is not yet integrated into an extendable immersive platform.  

There remains a greater need for experiential learning modes, remote access, and secured simulation spaces.

This tool will allow users throughout the country — **prospective students, admitted students, current students, and the general public** — to virtually explore the university's academic, administrative, and cultural spaces, accessing key information in an immersive and engaging way.  

By incorporating **gamification elements, multiplayer interaction, and educational resources**, the system will:

- Improve outreach and orientation processes.
- Democratize access to the university experience.
- Promote **inclusion, equity, and a sense of belonging**.

Furthermore, it represents a **strategic step** towards integrating metaverse technologies into public education, positioning UCR as a **pioneer in educational innovation in the region**.

## Environment Analysis

### Business Strategy

ThemePark@UCR aligns with the University of Costa Rica’s strategic goal to universalize access to higher education and promote innovation in teaching and outreach. By leveraging Web and Virtual Reality technologies, the university seeks to bridge geographical and informational gaps, making the campus experience accessible to all prospective and current students, regardless of location. The project positions UCR as a regional leader in immersive educational experiences, supporting institutional values of inclusion, equity, and knowledge sharing.

### System Objectives

- Provide an immersive, interactive platform that replicates the university campus and its academic, cultural, and administrative spaces.  
- Facilitate orientation and outreach for prospective and newly admitted students, helping them visualize and connect with university life before enrollment.  
- Support current students, faculty, and staff with virtual access to campus resources, services, and collaborative learning environments.  
- Enable researchers to study user interactions and behaviors in a secure, shared environment.  
- Integrate gamification and multiplayer features to foster engagement, collaboration, and a sense of community.  
- Ensure scalability, security, and accessibility for a diverse user base.  

### Expected Usage

The system is expected to be used by:  

- Prospective students exploring academic programs and campus life.  
- Newly admitted students familiarizing themselves with facilities and services.  
- Current students accessing resources, participating in virtual events, and collaborating with peers.  
- Faculty and staff supporting academic and administrative activities.  
- Researchers analyzing user interactions and educational outcomes.  
- General public interested in the university’s offerings and impact.  

### Legacy Systems

So far, there are some 2D virtual maps accessible through the UCR website, but they are not interactive and do not provide an immersive experience to users. The aforementioned maps can be found in the following links:  

- [UCR General Map](https://www.ucr.ac.cr/mapas/)  
- [Rodrigo Facio Campus Map](https://www.ucr.ac.cr/mapas/sede-central/)  

These two maps include the main buildings and roads of each campus, as well as some tags with basic information of each part, but they do not allow users to navigate through the campus or interact with the environment in any way.  

There is also the **Mediación Virtual system** ([mediacionvirtual.ucr.ac.cr](https://mediacionvirtual.ucr.ac.cr/)), which is a platform that provides online courses and resources for students and teachers of the UCR. However, this system is limited to Moodle-like course functionality and does not provide a 3D or VR experience.  

### Regulatory Aspects

The development and deployment of the ThemePark@UCR system must comply with various regulatory aspects in terms of data protection, accessibility, and intellectual property. It is important to ensure that the system adheres to the General Data Protection Law (Law No. 8968) of Costa Rica, which regulates the collection, use, and storage of personal data. This includes obtaining user consent for data collection and ensuring that sensitive information is adequately protected.  

Additionally, the system must adhere to the following guidelines of the Centro de Informática:  
[CI-ADS-L01 Lineamiento general para la gestión de seguridad de la información en los sistemas de información V2.0 (PDF)](https://ci.ucr.ac.cr/sites/default/files/2023-12/CI-ADS-L01%20Lineamiento%20general%20para%20la%20gestio%CC%81n%20de%20seguridad%20de%20la%20informacio%CC%81n%20en%20los%20sistemas%20de%20informacio%CC%81n%20V2.0.pdf)  

### Business Assumptions and Constraints

- The system must be implemented as a Web/VR application that is accessible from various devices.  
- The system must be designed to handle a large number of concurrent users, as it is expected to be used by prospective students, current students, faculty members, and visitors.  
- The system must also be scalable and adaptable to future technological advancements in VR and web technologies.  
- It should also be integrated with a relational database to store user data, content, and interactions.  
- The solution must follow iterative and incremental development methodologies, in this case SCRUM, for four defined sprints.  

### Existing Solutions

In the industry, several companies have developed VR experiences that serve as benchmarks such as:  

- [Orlando Theme Park VR](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1373810/Orlando_Theme_Park_VR__Roller_Coaster_and_Rides/)  
- [Theme Park Simulator: Rollercoaster Paradise](https://www.meta.com/experiences/theme-park-simulator-rollercoaster-paradise/3795573030551839/)  
- [Summer Funland](https://store.steampowered.com/app/780280/Summer_Funland/)  

These solutions offer immersive experiences that allow users to explore virtual environments and interact with various elements. They often include features such as realistic graphics, sound effects, and interactive gameplay to enhance user engagement.  

Focusing on immersive learning experiences, **Meta** has worked on projects like [Meta for Education](https://forwork.meta.com/meta-for-education/), which aims to provide educational institutions with VR tools to create interactive learning environments. This platform allows educators to design virtual classrooms, conduct immersive lessons, and facilitate collaborative learning experiences.  

However, up to this date, there are no known solutions specifically designed for Costa Rica and much less for the University of Costa Rica that offer a comprehensive virtual representation of a university campus with interactive features and gamification elements.

## External Systems Integration

External systems that the product may interact with:
### Identity Provider (IdP) for authentication and authorization
- Authentication and authorization of users using standars is a requirement for developing a secure system, since it's essential to ensure that all user credentials are managed securely and that access to the system is properly controlled.

### VR engines and platforms for assets and media delivery (e.g., Unity, Unreal Engine, WebXR).
- To achieve the goal of developing a VR-based educational platform, it's essential to integrate with established VR engines and platforms. This will allow the system to leverage existing technologies for creating, managing, and delivering VR content effectively.

### Learning Management Systems (LMS) to link VR experiences with courses.
- This integration will enable seamless linking of VR experiences with existing courses, allowing educators to enhance their curriculum with immersive content.

### Geospatial Data Providers (Maps-like) for location-based VR content.
- Integrating with geospatial data providers will allow the system to offer location-based VR content, enhancing the educational experience by providing contextually relevant information.

### External systems for users' chat-based interactions.
- In order to facilitate communication and collaboration among users, integrating with external chat systems will enable real-time interactions within the environment.

### Realtime data sources for dynamic content updates.
- With this integration, the system will be able to provide content updates, ensuring that users have access to the most current information during their VR experiences.

## Modules and Epics

### Ingeniería del Pozole: Users' information management

**Modules**:
- Create, read, update, and delete users. 
- Login and authentication.
- Profile management: addition, deletion, modification.
- Role-based access control.
- Privileges management.
- Multiplayer gamification system.

**Epics**
- As a user, I want to create an account so that I can access the platform.
- As a user, I want to interact with other users in a multiplayer environment so that I can collaborate and learn together.
- As an administrator, I want to manage user information so that I can keep correct and updated data in the system.
- As an administrator, I want to manage buildings information so that I can keep correct and updated data in the system.

Every user will have a profile with their information, including their role (student, teacher, admin). 
Depending on the role, users will have different privileges and access levels, through which they will be able to visit the platform's features.

### DevOops: Buildings' information management
**Modules**:
- Create, Read, Update, Delete, List (CRUDL) operations for buildings.
- Building access control based on user roles.
- Integration with AI agent for building information retrieval.
- Building visualization in 3D environment.

**Epics**
- As an administrator, I want to create a building so that I can register new buildings data in the system.
- As an administrator, I want to list the buildings so that I can see which buildings are in the campus.
- As an administrator, I want the AI agent integrated into the system so that I can interact with it through a defined interface and retrieve relevant building information.

Every building will have a color, dimensions (width, height, depth) and location (x, y, z coordinates).

### PIBIDS Solutions: Interactive Components information management
**Modules:**
* Create, Read, Update, Delete (CRUD) operations for interactive components.
* Create, Read, Update, Delete (CRUD) operations for interactive components with AI agent.
* Interactive components linking with Learning Spaces.

**Epics:**
* As a user I want to interact with whiteboards in a 3D environment so that I can have a more immersive and enjoyable experience.
* As a user, I want to be able to interact with projectors in a 3D environment so that I can have a more immersive and enjoyable experience.
* As a user, I want to be able to interact with learning components in a 3D environment so that I can have a more immersive and enjoyable experience.
* As a system administrator, I want to be able to manage interactive components so that I can ensure they are functioning properly and meet the needs of users.

### SQL Tilinos: Learning Spaces information management

**Modules:**
* Create, Read, Update, Delete, List (CRUDL) operations for Learning Spaces.
* Create, Read, Update, Delete, List (CRUDL) operations for Learning Spaces using an AI agent.
* Learning spaces linking with Buildings.
* Learning spaces linking with Interactive Components.
* Learning spaces access control based on user roles.
* Learning space visualization in 3D environment.

**Epics:**
* As a system administrator I want to manage information about laboratories so that they can be used in the system in the future.
* As a system administrator I want to manage information about laboratories using an AI agent so that they can be used in the system in the future.
* As a system administrator I want to manage information about classrooms so that they can be used in the system in the future.

## Non-functional Requirements

### Performance 
- The system should be able to handle a specified amount of concurrent users without significant performance degradation.
- The system should respond to user actions within a specified time frame. 
- The system should be optimized for low latency to ensure a smooth user experience.
- The VR environment should load within a specified time frame, based on expected visual quality and complexity.

### Scalability
- The system should be designed to scale horizontally to accommodate an increasing number of users and data.
- The system should be able to handle peak loads during high-traffic periods without performance degradation.

### Security
- The system should implement authentication and authorization mechanisms to protect user data and ensure that only authorized users can access certain features.
- The system should recover unexpected failures, such as crashes or data corruption, to ensure data integrity and availability.

### Usability and Accessibility
- The system should have an intuitive user interface that is easy to navigate for users of varying technical proficiency.
- The system should have an interface based on the institution 's branding guidelines.

### Maintainability
- The system should be designed with modularity in mind to facilitate easier maintenance and updates.
- The system should have comprehensive documentation to assist developers in understanding and maintaining the codebase.
- The system shoudl follow a clean arquitecture to ensure separation of concerns, in order to spot errors or rearrangements faster.
